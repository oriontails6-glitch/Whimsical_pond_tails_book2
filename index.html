The Egg, the Crew, and the Boss Who Was on Vacation — Book Two

Book Two

The Egg, the Crew
& the Absentee Boss

A missing egg. A chaotic underwater rescue squad.
And the actual reason nobody was supervising them.

Page 1

The Thing in the Dark Was… Kind of a Lot

Romeo and The TWINNS braced for the worst as the shadow surfaced: The Killer Shark (Assassin), all teeth and drama, sharpening his fangs on a rock. "I'VE. BEEN. WATCHING. YOU," he boomed, in a voice clearly rehearsed in a mirror.

Then the rock let out a tiny, humiliating squeak, and the whole villain-monologue collapsed. He tried again, 40% less confident, and asked if anyone had seen a small floating egg — because he'd "definitely not" been keeping it warm under one fin out of boredom. Definitely not.

Page 2

Pollleh Senses Something (Probably)

Before anyone could ask what he meant, Pollleh, the pond's fortune-telling octopus, drifted in on dramatic bubbles, tentacles waving like she was conducting an orchestra that did not exist. "I sense… great danger," she announced. "…Or possibly snacks. The signs are unclear today."

After a few wildly wrong guesses, she landed on something real: an egg had gone missing, swept off in the chaos of the Great Breakfast Committee Chase. "It must be returned before the tide turns," she declared, "or… something bad. I forget what. But it felt serious." Romeo and the twins exchanged horrified looks. An egg. Missing. From THEIR pond.

Page 3

Somebody Do a Headcount (And Jazzie Opens an App)

On the surface, Mama Duck sat back on her nest — and froze. Counted again. Counted a third time. Zero eggs. The whole shore erupted. Cool yet evil uncle (Akuku) declared it "an inside job" and flipped to a fresh notebook page like he'd been waiting his whole life for this.

Golden-Winged cool Aunt (Jazzie), mostly for something to do with her nerves, downloaded an app called PondCupid and built Mama Duck a dating profile without asking — interests: "twig discipline, emotional intensity, mild betrayal recovery." Ruthless Falcon Aunt just questioned everyone's sanity. Meanwhile the actual egg had simply rolled off the nest and sunk somewhere none of them could follow.

Page 4

Meet the Entire Search Party (All at Once, Buckle Up)

Down in the reef, a search party assembled with far more confidence than competence. Mexican Opossum-Fish announced he'd track the egg via "instincts" he was clearly inventing live. The Overpowered Clownfish (Nemo) declared his "Lucky Fin" was tingling — sign, or indigestion, unclear.

The Blue Wizard Fish (Phantom) yelled "EXPELLIARMUS!" at seaweed, repeatedly, achieving nothing. Dramatic Mermaid (Haillily) arrived on a tiny bicycle gasping this "changes everything." The Pirate Puffer guarded his treasure, tense for no reason. And weaving through it all, stealing a crystal just to hide it somewhere else for fun, was Xeroxx the Mischievous Orca — technically searching, mostly a one-orca chaos engine.

Page 5

Found It (Thanks to a Prank Gone Right)

Xeroxx, bored of the search, splash-bombed The Pirate Puffer in the face for fun. Startled out of his wits, the puffer inflated into a perfect spiky sphere and rocketed straight up — into a hidden cave ledge nobody had checked. Wedged safely between two rocks: the missing egg.

"IT WAS ME," announced the Puffer, still upside-down, taking full credit for a discovery achieved purely by getting pranked into a wall. Xeroxx, delighted, did not correct him — he was already hiding the stolen crystal in that same ledge. The Killer Shark, keen to reclaim some dignity, lifted the egg out with surprising gentleness, and the crew swam for the surface.

Page 6

The Whole Shore Loses Its Mind (In a Good Way)

Romeo and the Twinns burst up first, shouting that help was coming, just as the crew crested the surface with the egg held aloft. Mama Duck let out a sound nobody had ever heard from her — somewhere between a sob and a fire alarm — and waded in so fast she nearly flattened Fierce Grandma (Serratia), who for once didn't scold anyone about it.

The Killer Shark, with great unnecessary ceremony, presented the egg back personally and delivered one final line — "I've. Been. Watching. Over. It." — which landed considerably better this time, mostly because it was true and no squeaky rock was around to ruin it.

Page 7

One Big, Loud Family (Whose Actual Boss Had No Idea)

That night, the newly merged pond-and-reef family threw the loudest celebration the water had ever seen — tacos from Mexican Opossum-Fish, Dramatic Mermaid declaring six "best nights of her life," and Xeroxx sneaking bites off every plate without getting caught once. Mama Duck, egg tucked beneath her, invited the entire fish family to stay. Nobody argued.

What none of them knew: every fish in that crew technically had a boss — the reigning overlord of the underwater world — who'd simply been on an extended vacation this whole time, blissfully unaware his staff had unionized with a duck family and rescued an egg without consulting him once.

Page 8

The Boss Comes Back (Not Mad. Just Disappointed. Also Mad.)

Don Pengucci — sharp suit, sunglasses, permanent scowl — returned from his seventh vacation of the year to find his entire staff had formed a taco alliance with a duck family. The Killer Shark explained nervously that "it felt weird texting you mid-retreat." Pollleh tried to sense he wasn't upset, which he immediately, loudly, was.

He sighed the longest sigh in pond history, pulled out a pre-written resignation scroll he clearly kept on hand for exactly this, and — as always — put it right back without submitting it. Meanwhile, Golden-Winged cool Aunt's PondCupid experiment had finally produced a match: a mysterious, well-dressed penguin who replied in one word and somehow still made Mama Duck laugh.

Page 9

Wait. THAT'S the Boss?

Unable to stand the mystery, Don Pengucci announced he'd visit in person, citing "strictly professional supervision of my wayward staff." He arrived in a small, absurdly polished shell-boat, sunglasses on despite the cloudy sky. Waiting for him — badly disguised as reeds, bushes, and in cool yet evil uncle's case, a suspiciously duck-shaped rock — was the entire maternal family.

Mama Duck stepped forward. Don Pengucci stepped forward. Both froze. "You're… the fish boss," she said slowly. "You're the nest lady," he replied, equally slowly. Somewhere behind a reed, The Killer Shark whispered "oh no" loud enough for the whole shore to hear, while Fierce Grandma muttered she "approved, tentatively, pending a full background check."

Page 10

CLIFFHANGER: The Egg Chooses This Exact Moment

Right as the awkward silence between Mama Duck and Don Pengucci broke into a real laugh, and right as he pulled out his resignation scroll — again, unconvincingly — to declare he was "done with the underworld thing for good"... a small, unmistakable sound came from the nest behind her.

Crack.

The whole pond went silent. Mama Duck spun around so fast three feathers achieved liftoff on their own. The egg, home for barely a day, had chosen this exact, maximally dramatic second to begin hatching.

Will Don Pengucci actually stay this time? What's about to crack out of that egg? And does anyone still believe his resignation scroll?
